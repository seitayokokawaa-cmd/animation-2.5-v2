# Audio attribution

All sound effects and music beds in MotionForge films are **original,
procedurally synthesized** by this repository's engine
(`packages/render/src/sfx.ts` and `packages/render/src/music.ts`,
ADR-0009): pure seeded DSP, no recorded samples, no third-party audio.

To the extent possible under law, the authors dedicate the synthesized
audio output to the **public domain (CC0 1.0)** — use it for any purpose,
no permission needed.

Narration audio is produced per-film by the configured TTS engine and is
governed by that engine's terms (the offline mock engine's output is CC0
as above).
